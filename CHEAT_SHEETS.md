## CHEAT SHEET #1: Water Purification Quick Guide
### Emergency Water Treatment Methods

**BOILING (Most Reliable)**
- Bring to rolling boil: 1 minute
- Above 6,500 ft elevation: 3 minutes
- Kills: ALL bacteria, viruses, protozoa
- Does NOT remove: Chemicals, sediment

**BLEACH (Cheap & Effective)**
- Use UNSCENTED bleach only (5-9% sodium hypochlorite)
- CLEAR water: 8 drops (1/8 tsp) per gallon
- CLOUDY water: 16 drops (1/4 tsp) per gallon
- Mix well, wait 30 minutes
- Should smell slightly of chlorine
- If no smell, repeat dose and wait 15 min
- Does NOT remove: Sediment, chemicals

**TABLETS**
- Iodine or chlorine dioxide
- Follow package directions (usually 1 tab/liter)
- Wait 30 minutes minimum
- May need taste-neutralizer tablet

**SOLAR DISINFECTION (SODIS)**
- Clear plastic bottles only
- Fill with clear water
- Lay in direct sunlight
- 6 hours (sunny) or 2 days (cloudy)
- FREE method!

**FILTERS**
- Personal (LifeStraw): 1,000-4,000 liters
- Gravity (bag): 2-10 liters per fill
- Ceramic (Berkey): 1,000-5,000 gallons
- Check filter specs for what it removes

**WATER NEEDS**
- Minimum: 1 gallon/person/day
- Comfortable: 2-5 gallons/person/day
- 72 hours: 3 gallons minimum
- 2 weeks: 14 gallons minimum

**EMERGENCY SOURCES**
- Water heater tank: 30-50 gallons
- Toilet tank (NOT bowl): 2-3 gallons
- Pipes: Drain from lowest point
- Ice cubes: Melt for water
- Canned vegetables: Liquid is safe

## CHEAT SHEET #2: Calorie & Nutrition Quick Guide
### Daily Food Requirements

**CALORIE NEEDS**
- Adult Male: 2,000-2,500 calories/day
- Adult Female: 1,800-2,200 calories/day
- Teenager (13-18): 2,200-2,800 calories/day
- Child (6-12): 1,600-2,000 calories/day
- Child (under 6): 1,000-1,400 calories/day
- Heavy activity: Add 500-1,000 calories
- Pregnancy: Add 300 calories
- Nursing: Add 500 calories

**SURVIVAL MINIMUM**
- Short-term (days): 800-1,200 calories/day
- Long-term: 1,500-2,000 calories/day minimum
- Below 1,200: Risk of malnutrition

**MACRONUTRIENTS**
- Carbohydrates: 45-65% of calories
- Protein: 10-35% of calories
- Fats: 20-35% of calories

**CRITICAL VITAMINS/MINERALS**
- Vitamin C: 75-90 mg/day (scurvy prevention)
- Vitamin A: 700-900 mcg/day (eyesight)
- Iron: 8-18 mg/day (anemia prevention)
- Calcium: 1,000-1,300 mg/day (bones)
- B Vitamins: Varies (energy, nerves)
- Zinc: 8-11 mg/day (immune system)

**EMERGENCY FOOD PRIORITIES**
1. Carbs (energy): Rice, pasta, oats, flour
2. Protein: Beans, canned meat, peanut butter
3. Fats: Cooking oil, nuts, seeds
4. Salt: Critical for survival
5. Vitamins: Multivitamin supplement
6. Variety: Prevents deficiency

**SHELF LIFE**
- Honey: Indefinite
- Rice (white): 30 years
- Dried beans: 30 years
- Pasta: 30 years
- Flour: 5-10 years
- Canned goods: 2-5 years
- Dried fruit: 1-2 years

## CHEAT SHEET #3: First Aid Quick Reference
### Emergency Medical Response

**LIFE-THREATENING (Call 911)**
- Not breathing: Start CPR immediately
- Severe bleeding: Apply direct pressure
- Chest pain: Aspirin + call 911
- Allergic reaction: EpiPen + call 911
- Head injury + unconscious: Call 911
- Burns (large area): Cool + call 911

**ABCs OF FIRST AID**
- **A**irway: Is it clear?
- **B**reathing: Are they breathing?
- **C**irculation: Do they have a pulse?

**BLEEDING**
- Direct pressure: Use clean cloth
- Elevate wound above heart
- Pressure points if needed
- Tourniquet: LAST RESORT only
- Don't remove impaled objects

**BURNS**
- Cool (not cold) water: 10-20 minutes
- Cover with clean, dry cloth
- DO NOT use ice
- DO NOT pop blisters
- Seek medical attention if severe

**FRACTURES**
- Immobilize injured area
- Don't try to straighten
- Apply ice (20 min on, 20 min off)
- Elevate if possible
- Seek medical attention

**SHOCK**
- Lay person down
- Elevate feet 12 inches
- Keep warm with blanket
- DO NOT give food/water
- Monitor breathing

**VITAL SIGNS (Normal)**
- Pulse: 60-100 beats/minute
- Breathing: 12-20 breaths/minute
- Temperature: 97-99°F (36-37°C)
- Blood pressure: 120/80 mmHg

**BASIC KIT CONTENTS**
- Bandages (various sizes)
- Gauze pads (4×4)
- Medical tape
- Antibiotic ointment
- Pain reliever (ibuprofen, acetaminophen)
- Tweezers, scissors
- Disposable gloves
- CPR face shield
- Thermometer
- Emergency blanket

## CHEAT SHEET #4: Emergency Shelter & Temperature
### Survival in Extreme Conditions

**RULE OF THREES**
- 3 minutes: Without air
- 3 hours: Without shelter (extreme weather)
- 3 days: Without water
- 3 weeks: Without food

**HYPOTHERMIA PREVENTION**
- Stay dry (wet = cold)
- Layer clothing (base, mid, outer)
- Cover head (30% heat loss)
- Cover extremities (hands, feet)
- Stay active (generate heat)
- Eat high-calorie food
- Emergency blanket (reflects 90% heat)

**HYPOTHERMIA TREATMENT**
- Get out of cold/wind
- Remove wet clothing
- Warm core first (chest, neck, head)
- Warm drinks if conscious
- DO NOT rub extremities
- Seek medical attention

**HEAT EXHAUSTION PREVENTION**
- Drink water frequently
- Rest in shade
- Light-colored clothing
- Wet clothing/bandana on neck
- Avoid midday activity

**HEAT EXHAUSTION TREATMENT**
- Move to cool location
- Remove excess clothing
- Cool with water/fanning
- Drink water slowly
- Rest until recovered

**EMERGENCY SHELTER PRIORITIES**
1. Protection from wind
2. Protection from precipitation
3. Insulation from ground
4. Heat retention

**TARP SHELTER (5 minutes)**
- A-frame: String rope, drape tarp, stake corners
- Lean-to: One side staked high, other side low
- Diamond: Stake center, stretch corners

**LAYERING SYSTEM**
- Base: Wicks moisture (NOT cotton)
- Mid: Insulates (fleece, down)
- Outer: Protects from wind/rain
- Adjust layers before you sweat

## CHEAT SHEET #7: Water Storage Quick Math
### How Much Do You Need?

**BASIC CALCULATION**
- 1 gallon per person per day
- Multiply by number of days
- Multiply by number of people

**EXAMPLES**
- 1 person × 3 days = 3 gallons
- 4 people × 3 days = 12 gallons
- 4 people × 14 days = 56 gallons
- 4 people × 30 days = 120 gallons

**CONTAINER SIZES**
- 2-liter bottle = 0.5 gallon
- 5-gallon jug = 5 gallons (obviously)
- 55-gallon drum = 55 gallons
- Case of water (24 bottles) = 3 gallons
- Water heater tank = 30-50 gallons

**STORAGE SPACE**
- 3 gallons = 12 lbs = 1 case water
- 14 gallons = 56 lbs = 3 five-gallon jugs
- 30 gallons = 120 lbs = 6 five-gallon jugs
- 55 gallons = 220 lbs = 1 drum (HEAVY!)

**QUICK CONVERSIONS**
- 1 gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints = 16 cups
- 1 gallon = 3.78 liters
- 1 liter = 0.264 gallons
- 8 oz = 1 cup = 237 ml

## CHEAT SHEET #8: Food Rotation Schedule
### Prevent Waste, Stay Fresh

**ROTATION SYSTEM**
- FIFO: First In, First Out
- Date everything with marker
- Store new items in back
- Use oldest items first
- Check expiration dates monthly

**SHELF LIFE GUIDE**
**Indefinite (if stored properly):**
- White rice
- Honey
- Salt
- Sugar
- Dried beans
- Wheat (whole kernel)

**10-30 Years:**
- Freeze-dried meals
- Powdered milk
- Dried pasta
- Rolled oats

**5-10 Years:**
- Canned meats
- Canned vegetables
- Canned fruits
- Flour (vacuum sealed)

**2-5 Years:**
- Canned goods (typical)
- Peanut butter
- Cooking oil
- Crackers (sealed)

**1-2 Years:**
- Energy bars
- Dried fruit
- Nuts
- Granola

**6-12 Months:**
- Breakfast cereal
- Chips/snacks
- Coffee/tea

**ROTATION STRATEGY**
1. Buy extra of what you eat
2. Date with marker
3. Use oldest first
4. Replace as you use
5. Check every 6 months

**MONTHLY CHECK**
☐ Inspect for damage/rust
☐ Check expiration dates
☐ Rotate items
☐ Update inventory list
☐ Top off as needed

## CHEAT SHEET #9: Emergency Signals
### Get Rescued Faster

**INTERNATIONAL DISTRESS SIGNALS**

**SOS (Morse Code):**
- ... --- ... (3 short, 3 long, 3 short)
- Flash with light
- Sound with whistle
- Any pattern of 3 = distress

**GROUND-TO-AIR SIGNALS**
(Make large, visible from air)
- X = Need medical assistance
- F = Need food and water
- → = Proceeding in this direction
- LL = All is well
- V = Need assistance
- Use rocks, branches, bright clothing

**WHISTLE SIGNALS**
- 1 blast = "Where are you?"
- 2 blasts = "Come to me"
- 3 blasts = EMERGENCY
- Repeat every minute

**MIRROR SIGNAL**
- Reflect sunlight at aircraft/rescuers
- Flash 3 times, pause, repeat
- Can be seen for miles
- Use any reflective surface

**FIRE SIGNALS**
- 3 fires in triangle = distress
- Daytime: Add green branches for smoke
- Nighttime: Bright flames
- Space apart 100 feet

**AUDIO SIGNALS**
- 3 of anything = distress
- 3 gunshots
- 3 shouts
- 3 whistle blasts
- 3 horn honks

**VISUAL SIGNALS**
- Bright clothing waved
- Flashlight at night
- Reflective mirror
- Flares (if available)
- Orange smoke (daytime)
