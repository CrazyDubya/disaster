# QUICK START - Emergency Preparedness in 30 Minutes

**Complete beginner? Start here. This is your fastest path to basic preparedness.**

---

## ⚡ The Absolute Minimum (Do This TODAY - 30 Minutes)

### Step 1: Water (10 minutes)
**Goal: 3 days of drinking water**

**Action:**
1. Go to your kitchen
2. Find empty 2-liter soda bottles (or buy bottled water)
3. Calculate: 1 gallon = 4 liters per person for 3 days
4. Fill bottles with tap water OR buy bottled water
5. Store in cool, dark place

**Cost: $0-15**
**You now have:** Basic hydration for 72 hours ✅

### Step 2: Food (10 minutes)
**Goal: 3 days of ready-to-eat food**

**Action:**
1. Check your pantry for:
   - Canned soup, beans, vegetables
   - Peanut butter, crackers
   - Energy bars, granola
   - Anything edible without cooking
2. Put aside enough for 3 days
3. Include a manual can opener!

**Cost: $0-30** (if you already have canned goods)
**You now have:** Basic food for 72 hours ✅

### Step 3: Light & Communication (5 minutes)
**Goal: See in the dark, get information**

**Action:**
1. Find: Flashlight + batteries
2. Find: Your phone charger
3. Optional: Buy a cheap battery-powered radio

**Cost: $0-20**
**You now have:** Light and communication ✅

### Step 4: First Aid (5 minutes)
**Goal: Handle minor injuries**

**Action:**
1. Check for existing first aid kit
2. OR gather: Band-aids, pain reliever, antibiotic ointment
3. Add any prescription medications (3-day supply)

**Cost: $0-25**
**You now have:** Basic medical supplies ✅

## 🚨 Common Beginner Mistakes

### ❌ Don't Do This:
- **Don't wait** for "the perfect kit" - start with basics
- **Don't buy expensive** pre-made kits - DIY is better and cheaper
- **Don't ignore water** - it's #1 priority
- **Don't forget medications** - especially prescriptions
- **Don't tell everyone** - operational security matters

### ✅ Do This Instead:
- **Start small** - even 3 days is better than nothing
- **Build gradually** - add items weekly
- **Focus on basics** - water, food, shelter, medical
- **Test your kit** - practice using everything
- **Keep it simple** - complicated = won't do it

## 💡 The 72-Hour Rule

**Why 72 hours?**
- Emergency services take 3 days to reach most areas after major disasters
- Stores run out of supplies in 24-48 hours
- You need self-sufficiency for the critical first days

**What you need:**
- Water: 3 gallons per person
- Food: 2,000-2,400 calories per day
- Shelter: Emergency blanket + weather protection
- Medical: Basic first aid + prescriptions
- Sanitation: Toilet paper, wet wipes, hand sanitizer

**Complete guide:** [TIMELINE_72_HOURS.md](TIMELINE_72_HOURS.md)

## 📋 The Essential 20 Items

**If you only get 20 things, get these:**

### Water & Food (6 items)
1. **Water** - 3 gallons per person (bottled or stored)
2. **Canned food** - Soup, beans, vegetables (9 cans minimum)
3. **Protein** - Peanut butter, tuna packets, jerky
4. **Energy bars** - Quick calories, long shelf life
5. **Can opener** - Manual, P-38 style
6. **Eating utensils** - Disposable or camping

### Shelter & Warmth (4 items)
7. **Emergency blanket** - Mylar/"space blanket" ($2-5)
8. **Tarp** - 8×10 minimum, multiple uses
9. **Paracord** - 50-100 feet
10. **Extra clothing** - Socks, underwear, jacket

### Medical (3 items)
11. **First aid kit** - Basic bandages, antiseptic
12. **Pain reliever** - Ibuprofen or acetaminophen
13. **Prescription meds** - 7-day supply minimum

### Tools & Light (4 items)
14. **Flashlight** - LED, extra batteries
15. **Radio** - Battery or hand-crank, NOAA weather
16. **Multi-tool** - Knife, pliers, screwdriver
17. **Lighter/matches** - Waterproof container

### Sanitation (3 items)
18. **Toilet paper** - 1 roll per person minimum
19. **Hand sanitizer** - 60%+ alcohol
20. **Garbage bags** - Heavy duty, multiple uses

**Total cost: $100-200**

## 🏠 Where to Store Everything

### Best Locations:
1. **Near main exit** - Easy to grab
2. **Closet by front door** - Accessible
3. **Under bed** - Out of way but reachable
4. **Hall closet** - Central location

### Avoid:
- ❌ Attic (too hot)
- ❌ Garage (temperature extremes)
- ❌ Basement (flooding risk)
- ❌ Far from exits

### Containers:
- Plastic tote with lid (waterproof)
- Backpack (portable)
- Rolling suitcase (wheels help)
- 5-gallon bucket (cheap, durable)

## ❓ FAQ for Absolute Beginners

**Q: Is this for crazy preppers?**
A: No! This is basic common sense. You have car insurance, right? Same idea.

**Q: How long will this take?**
A: 30 minutes for basics, 2 hours for complete 72-hour kit.

**Q: How much will it cost?**
A: $0-200 depending on what you have. Most people spend $100-150.

**Q: What if I live in an apartment?**
A: Perfect! Smaller space = easier to prepare. Focus on compact items.

**Q: What disaster am I preparing for?**
A: All of them. This works for power outages, storms, floods, earthquakes, etc.

**Q: Do I need guns?**
A: Not for basic preparedness. Focus on water, food, shelter, medical first.

**Q: What about my kids?**
A: Each person needs their own supplies. Include comfort items (toys, books).

**Q: What about my pets?**
A: Yes! Add 3 days of pet food, water, medications, carrier.
