## Understanding the Timeline Approach

Different disaster scenarios require different levels of preparedness:

### 🔴 72 Hours (Immediate Emergency)
**Scenarios:** Natural disasters, sudden evacuations, power outages
**Focus:** Survival basics - water, food, shelter, first aid
**Portability:** High - must be grab-and-go ready
**Key Question:** "Can I survive for 3 days without outside help?"

### 🟠 2 Weeks (Extended Emergency)
**Scenarios:** Major storms, regional disasters, supply chain disruptions
**Focus:** Extended supplies, sanitation, communication
**Portability:** Medium - some items stored at home
**Key Question:** "Can my household function for 2 weeks independently?"

### 🟡 1 Month (Short-Term Self-Sufficiency)
**Scenarios:** Economic disruption, prolonged utility failures, pandemics
**Focus:** Food rotation, water storage, basic medical care
**Portability:** Low - primarily home storage
**Key Question:** "Can we maintain health and sanitation for 30 days?"

### 🟢 3-6 Months (Medium-Term Sustainability)
**Scenarios:** Economic collapse, extended supply chain failure, long-term displacement
**Focus:** Food variety, nutrition balance, mental health, skill development
**Portability:** None - established home or retreat storage
**Key Question:** "Can we sustain ourselves with minimal external resources?"

### 🔵 1 Year (Long-Term Preparedness)
**Scenarios:** Major economic depression, prolonged civil unrest, environmental catastrophe
**Focus:** Bulk staples, gardening, preservation, self-sufficiency skills
**Portability:** None - requires significant storage space
**Key Question:** "Can we produce and preserve our own resources?"

### 🟣 Multi-Year (Post-Apocalyptic/Societal Collapse)
**Scenarios:** Complete infrastructure failure, extreme climate change, societal breakdown
**Focus:** Agriculture, renewable energy, community building, complete self-sufficiency
**Portability:** None - permanent location with land
**Key Question:** "Can we rebuild sustainable living systems?"

## Critical Needs Across All Timelines

### Water
- **72hrs:** 1 gallon/person/day (3 gallons minimum)
- **2 weeks:** 14 gallons/person + purification methods
- **1 month:** 30 gallons/person + storage rotation + purification
- **3-6 months:** Water source identification, advanced filtration
- **1 year:** Well/spring access, rain catchment systems
- **Multi-year:** Sustainable water source, irrigation systems

### Food (Calories per day: 1,200-2,400 depending on age/activity)
- **72hrs:** Ready-to-eat meals, no cooking required
- **2 weeks:** Mix of fresh, canned, and dry goods
- **1 month:** Canned goods, rice, pasta, rotation system
- **3-6 months:** Bulk staples, variety for nutrition, comfort foods
- **1 year:** Bulk wheat, beans, rice, salt, cooking oil
- **Multi-year:** Gardening, seed saving, food production

### Shelter
- **72hrs:** Emergency blankets, tarp, portable shelter
- **2 weeks:** Home winterization, backup heating/cooling
- **1 month:** Insulation, weatherproofing, temperature control
- **3-6 months:** Alternative heating, structural repairs
- **1 year:** Sustainable heating/cooling solutions
- **Multi-year:** Off-grid housing, renewable systems

### Sanitation
- **72hrs:** Portable toilet, toilet paper, hand sanitizer
- **2 weeks:** Waste disposal system, hygiene supplies
- **1 month:** Composting toilet, greywater system
- **3-6 months:** Established sanitation routine, soap making
- **1 year:** Sustainable hygiene production
- **Multi-year:** Complete waste management system

### Medical
- **72hrs:** First aid kit, basic medications
- **2 weeks:** Extended medical supplies, prescription stockpile
- **1 month:** Comprehensive first aid, dental supplies
- **3-6 months:** Natural remedies, medical skill development
- **1 year:** Medicinal herb garden, advanced medical training
- **Multi-year:** Traditional medicine, injury prevention

### Energy
- **72hrs:** Batteries, flashlights, hand-crank radio
- **2 weeks:** Battery bank, small solar charger
- **1 month:** Generator, fuel storage
- **3-6 months:** Solar panels, battery backup
- **1 year:** Off-grid power system
- **Multi-year:** Renewable energy infrastructure

### Clothing & Temperature
- **72hrs:** Weather-appropriate clothing, emergency blankets
- **2 weeks:** Layering system, backup clothing
- **1 month:** Seasonal clothing rotation, repair supplies
- **3-6 months:** Clothing repair/modification skills
- **1 year:** Textile crafting, preservation techniques
- **Multi-year:** Textile production (spinning, weaving)

### Nutrition (Preventing Deficiencies)
- **72hrs:** Balanced emergency rations
- **2 weeks:** Multivitamins, varied food sources
- **1 month:** Vitamin C, calcium, iron supplementation
- **3-6 months:** Diverse food storage, fermented foods
- **1 year:** Complete nutrition planning, sprouting
- **Multi-year:** Diverse agriculture, animal products

## Special Considerations

### Family Planning
- **Infants:** Formula, diapers, baby food (see [Food Guide](TOPIC_FOOD.md))
- **Elderly:** Medications, mobility aids, special dietary needs
- **Pets:** Food, water, medications, carriers
- **Medical Conditions:** Extended medication supply, medical equipment

### Climate Considerations
- **Cold Climates:** Heating, insulation, cold-weather clothing
- **Hot Climates:** Cooling, sun protection, increased water needs
- **Humid Climates:** Mold prevention, moisture control, food preservation
- **Arid Climates:** Water conservation, dust protection

### Location Type
- **Urban:** Limited space, apartment-friendly solutions, community resources
- **Suburban:** Moderate space, vehicle access, neighborhood cooperation
- **Rural:** Land access, self-sufficiency, isolation challenges
