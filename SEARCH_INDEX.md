## How to Use This Index

1. **Use your browser's search** (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F)
2. **Search for your keyword** (e.g., "bleach", "calories", "shelter")
3. **Find the resource** with file location and section
4. **Click the link** to jump directly to the information
