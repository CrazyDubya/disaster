**LOCATION_GUIDES.md** (24KB) ✨ NEW THIS SESSION
- Comprehensive analysis of 4 environments:
  - Urban (Cities/Apartments) - 15KB
  - Suburban (Single-Family Homes) - 12KB
  - Rural (Countryside) - 10KB

**Quick Comparison Matrix:**
- 12 critical factors compared
- Population density, space availability, water access
- Food storage capacity, gardening potential
- Emergency services response time
- Resource scarcity timeline
- Self-sufficiency feasibility
- Evacuation challenges
- Community support
- Climate control

**Urban Environment (Deep Coverage):**
- Space constraints (500-1,000 sq ft living)
- Infrastructure 100% dependence
- High population = rapid depletion
- Solutions: Water bricks, vertical storage, OPSEC
- Cooking: Camp stove SAFELY (ventilation critical)
- Heating: NEVER charcoal indoors = CO death
- Sanitation: Bucket toilet system
- Gardening: Container, balcony, sprouts
- Security: Don't advertise preps
- Evacuation: Bug out early, on foot plan

**Suburban Environment:**
- Advantages: 1,500-3,000 sq ft, garage, yard
- Water: 55-gal drums, rainwater collection
- Food: 6-12 month supply feasible
- Garden: 400-1,000 sq ft can feed family
- Solar: Roof space available
- Generator: 3,000-7,000W portable
- Chickens: 3-6 hens = 12-30 eggs/week
- Community: Neighborhood organizing

**Rural Environment:**
- Advantages: 1-40+ acres, well water, livestock
- Challenges: Isolation (emergency services 30-60+ min)
- Water: Hand pump backup CRITICAL
- Food: Large garden (2,000-10,000 sq ft)
- Livestock: Chickens, goats, pigs, cattle
- Hunting: Big game, fishing, foraging
- Preservation: Root cellar, canning, dehydrating
- Energy: Off-grid solar feasible
- Heating: Wood stove + 3-6 cords/year

**Mountain Environment:**
- Challenges: 7,000-12,000+ ft altitude
- Short season: June-August only (60 days!)
- Extreme weather: -20°F to -40°F
- Snow: 10-30 feet accumulation
- Isolation: Hours from services, no cell
- Water: Must not freeze (indoor storage)
- Food: 3+ months stored (snowed in)
- Heating: 6-10 cords/winter = life/death
- Medical: WFR training minimum (hospital 1-3 hrs)
- Communication: Satellite phone, HAM radio

**Rural Self-Reliance:**

- Livestock independence
- Community tight-knit but distant
